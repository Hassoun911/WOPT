# WOPT Qur’an Experience

The `/quran` route is the Qur’an reading, listening, search, bookmark, and memorization workspace for the Windsor Prayer Times PWA.

## Included

- Mushaf-inspired Arabic reading layout with inline ayah markers.
- Surah navigation and Arabic/English search.
- Reciter selection and ayah/selection playback.
- Translation and transliteration controls.
- Ayah selection for focused memorization.
- Read, first-word hint, and recall stages.
- Browser-based Arabic recitation sequence comparison.
- Bookmarks and exact local resume state including chapter, ayah, page, and tapped word.
- Reader font size, line spacing, and paper/sepia/night themes.
- Mobile navigation integration with Today, Qur’an, Alerts, and Settings.

Qur’an text is loaded from Qur’an content data and is never generated or rewritten by the AI study features.
